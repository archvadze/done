# Acumen Craft – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2025-07-22_

---

## 1. Introduction

Acumen Craft respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, your rights, and how you can control your data on our platform and mobile applications.

---

## 2. What We Collect

We collect the following types of data:

- **Account Information:** Name, email, password, language, creative field, social/OAuth IDs.
- **Profile Data:** Bio, avatar, location, preferences, notification settings.
- **Content Data:** Uploaded artworks, comments, evaluations, chat messages, AI queries.
- **Payment Data:** Transaction and withdrawal history, payment processor IDs, wallet addresses.
- **Technical Data:** Device type, browser, IP address, cookies, app usage logs.
- **Analytics Data:** Usage statistics, event logs, error reports.
- **Support Data:** Tickets, helpdesk conversations, attachments.
- **Security Data:** 2FA secrets, security logs, session/device info.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Data

- To provide and improve the platform’s features and services.
- To authenticate, secure, and manage user accounts (including 2FA, OAuth).
- To process payments, withdrawals, and NFT/crypto transactions.
- To moderate content, handle copyright/infringement reports.
- To personalize experience, notifications, and recommendations.
- To analyze usage, troubleshoot, and improve the product.
- To provide support and communicate important updates.
- To comply with legal obligations and platform security.

---

## 4. Data Sharing

- **No sale of personal data.**
- Data is shared only as necessary with:  
  - Payment processors (Stripe, PayPal, crypto providers)
  - Cloud storage & hosting (AWS, GCP, etc.)
  - AI service providers (for chat/analysis, anonymized)
  - NFT/blockchain networks (public wallet, token metadata)
  - Legal authorities (as required by law)
- Third-party services are contractually obligated to secure your data.

---

## 5. International Transfers

- Data may be stored or processed in the EU, US, or other jurisdictions with adequate safeguards.
- Users in the EEA: Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and DPA available on request.

---

## 6. Data Retention

- Account data kept until profile deletion.
- Artworks/comments/chat retained as long as account is active, or as required by law.
- Payment & security logs retained for legal minimum periods (usually 5 years).
- Users can request deletion or export of their data at any time.

---

## 7. Your Rights

- Access, correct, or delete your data from profile settings or by request.
- Download/export your data (GDPR Art. 20, CCPA).
- Object to or restrict certain processing.
- Withdraw consent for notifications, analytics, or marketing.
- Erase your account and all personal data ("right to be forgotten").

---

## 8. Security

- All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.
- 2FA and strong password requirements.
- Security logs and anomaly detection.
- Security incidents are disclosed within 72 hours if required by law.

---

## 9. Children's Privacy

- Platform is not intended for children under 16.
- No knowingly collected or processed data of minors.

---

## 10. Changes to This Policy

- We may update this policy; material changes will be notified via email or in-app.
- Latest version always available at `/PRIVACY.md`.
