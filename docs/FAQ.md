# Acumen Craft – Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

_Last updated: 2025-07-22_

---

## General

### What is Acumen Craft?

Acumen Craft is a digital platform for creators and artists to share, evaluate, monetize, and protect their works using AI, NFT, and modern copyright tools.

### Who can join Acumen Craft?

Anyone! Artists, designers, photographers, musicians, AI creators, and fans are welcome. Both individuals and collectives are supported.

---

## Account & Access

### How do I create an account?

Visit [acumencraft.com](https://acumencraft.com) and click "Sign Up". You can register using email or social login (Google, Apple, Facebook).

### Can I use two-factor authentication?

Yes. Enable 2FA from your profile settings for enhanced account security.

### How do I delete my account?

Go to Account Settings → Delete Account. All your data will be removed in accordance with our [Privacy Policy](./docs/PRIVACY.md).

---

## Artworks & Evaluation

### What file types are supported?

- Images: JPG, PNG, GIF, SVG, WEBP
- Audio: MP3, WAV, OGG
- Video: MP4, WebM
- Other formats may be added upon request.

### How does artwork evaluation work?

Artworks can be evaluated by the community and AI along several criteria (technique, originality, composition, impact). Results contribute to an ACQ (Acuman Craft Quality) score.

### Can I upload AI-generated content?

Yes. You must mark the “AI-generated” flag during upload. AI art is welcome and discoverable.

---

## NFTs & Blockchain

### How do I mint my work as an NFT?

After uploading, click “Mint as NFT” and follow the on-screen instructions to connect your crypto wallet.

### Which blockchains are supported?

We currently support Ethereum and Polygon. Additional chains may be added in the future.

### Do I need crypto to join?

No. Crypto is only required for NFT minting or withdrawals in digital assets.

---

## Payments & Monetization

### How do I earn on Acumen Craft?

You can receive donations, sell artworks, and earn from NFT sales. We support Stripe, PayPal, and select cryptocurrencies.

### How do I withdraw earnings?

Go to Wallet → Withdraw, select your preferred payout method, and follow the prompts. Processing times vary by method.

### Are there fees?

Platform fees apply to sales and withdrawals; see [PRICING.md](./docs/PRICING.md) or the Fees section in your dashboard.

## Privacy & Security

### How is my data protected?

Data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We follow security best practices and comply with GDPR/CCPA. See [SECURITY.md](./docs/SECURITY.md).

### What if I find a security vulnerability?

Please report immediately to security@acumencraft.com. See [SECURITY.md](./docs/SECURITY.md) for responsible disclosure.

## Community & Moderation

### How do I report inappropriate content?

Use the “Report” button on any artwork or comment, or email support@acumencraft.com.

### Is there a code of conduct?

Yes, see [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](./docs/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) for our community guidelines.
