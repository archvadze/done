# Acumen Craft – Product Roadmap

_Last updated: 2025-07-22_

---

## Vision

Acumen Craft aims to be the leading platform for digital creators, artists, and their audiences, combining advanced copyright, AI, evaluation, NFT, and financial tools in a unified ecosystem.

---

## Roadmap at a Glance

| Quarter       | Milestone Type   | Highlights                                       |
|---------------|------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| Q3 2025       | Alpha            | Core MVP, internal testing, closed feedback loop |
| Q4 2025       | Beta/Public Test | User onboarding, payments, mobile beta           |
| Q1 2026       | Launch           | Public release, marketing, partnerships          |
| Q2-Q4 2026    | Growth           | Scaling, new features, internationalization      |

---

## Detailed Roadmap

### Q3 2025 — Alpha

- **Core Platform MVP**
  - User registration/login (email, OAuth, 2FA)
  - Artworks: upload, browse, edit, delete, multi-language support
  - Evaluations: manual/AI scoring, ACQ calculation
  - Comments, likes, basic notifications
  - Basic profile (avatar, bio, creative field)
  - Admin dashboard (moderation, reports)
- **Security & Privacy**
  - Encrypted storage, TLS everywhere, privacy controls
- **Internal QA**
  - Closed group testing, bug tracking, feedback refinement

---

### Q4 2025 — Beta/Public Test

- **Payments & Donations**
  - Stripe, PayPal integration (fiat)
  - Crypto payments (ETH, USDT, BTC)
  - Withdrawal system
- **NFT Functionality**
  - Mint artwork as NFT, connect wallet, view ownership
- **Community & Social**
  - User following, communities, group chat
  - Public/Private messaging, AI assistant
- **Mobile Beta**
  - Flutter/React Native app (core features)
  - Push notifications (FCM/APNs)
- **HelpDesk & Support**
  - FAQ, support tickets, help articles
- **Advanced Moderation**
  - Copyright/infringement workflow
  - Security logs, audit trails

---

### Q1 2026 — Public Launch

- **Full Platform Launch**
  - Public registration, open API
  - Full mobile support (App Store, Google Play)
  - Multilingual UI (EN, GE, DE, RU, ES)
  - Full content moderation suite
  - Advanced analytics (user, artwork, revenue)
  - Marketing campaigns, PR, first partnerships
- **Compliance**
  - GDPR/CCPA, PCI DSS, age checks, legal review

---

### Q2-Q4 2026 — Growth & Expansion

- **Ecosystem Expansion**
  - Marketplace for creative services
  - B2B API access, integrations (Figma, Behance, etc.)
  - Plugin/app marketplace (widgets, AI models)
  - Community events, contests, leaderboards
- **Internationalization**
  - More languages, local payment providers
  - Regional ambassadors, local partnerships
- **AI & Automation**
  - Smarter evaluation, fraud detection, content recommendations
  - AI-driven copyright detection, watermarking
- **Web3 & Future Tech**
  - Advanced NFT utilities (royalties, splits, unlockable content)
  - DAO/governance features for communities
  - On-chain reputation/identity

---

## Feature Backlog (2025-2026)

- Video streaming & live events
- API webhooks for all major events
- Advanced portfolio builder
- Granular notification settings (in-app, email, push)
- Accessibility improvements (WCAG compliance)
- Data portability and user exports
- White-label/enterprise version

---

## Key Metrics & Success Criteria

- User growth (DAU/MAU)
- Artwork uploads and engagement
- Payment/withdrawal volume
- NFT mints and secondary sales
- ACQ scores and evaluation activity
- Community participation

---

## Feedback & Iteration

- Continuous user feedback (in-app, support, Discord/Telegram)
- Open roadmap and voting for features
- Quarterly reviews and transparent updates
